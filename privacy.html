Privacy Notice

What this website collects about you, which is very little, in plain words. Effective September 6, 2026.

The short version. Never Watch iCarley is a parody website run by Recursis Holdings. It has no accounts and no forms. It counts page views without cookies, and — unless you turn it off below — it loads a Meta ad pixel. We don't sell your data, and a person answers info@recursisdigital.com.

What we collect and why

Nothing you type. There is nothing to type. The "Warn someone" button uses your phone's share sheet or copies text to your clipboard; that happens on your device and nothing is sent to us.

Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views and measure how fast the page loads. They are cookieless, don't build a profile of you, and don't follow you across other sites. We see aggregate numbers — pages, countries, device types, load times — not people. How Vercel handles it.

The ad pixel. This site loads the Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) so we can tell whether our ads bring people here, and show our ads to people who have visited. The pixel sets cookies in your browser and sends Meta the pages you view here, with your browser and IP details. For visitors in California we send Meta the "Limited Data Use" flag, which tells Meta to process that data under California's restrictions. Under California's definition this counts as "sharing" data for advertising, so we give you a real switch: the button below stops the pixel from loading on this browser, and we also honor your browser's Global Privacy Control signal automatically. Meta's privacy policy covers what Meta does with what it receives.

The store. shop.neverwatchicarley.com is hosted by Fourthwall, which is the merchant of record: Fourthwall takes your payment, prints, ships, and handles returns under its own privacy policy and terms (linked in the store footer). We can see order details in the store dashboard so we can help with a problem; we don't get your card number.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data, and we never hand it to data brokers.
  • We don't send email. We have no list to send it to.
  • This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
